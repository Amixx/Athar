# Notice for GNI-BIM sample corpus

This folder contains a small extracted subset of IFC model files from the GNI BIM Dataset.

## Source

- Dataset: **GNI BIM Dataset**
- Repository: <https://github.com/ZijianWang-ZW/GNI-BIM-Dataset>
- Zenodo DOI: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19722011>
- Zenodo record/version DOI used by the upstream citation: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19722012>
- Publisher / institution: Technical University of Munich, Georg Nemetschek Institute (GNI)
